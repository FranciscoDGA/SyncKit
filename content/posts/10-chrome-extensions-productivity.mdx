Every extension on this list passed a strict test: **measurable time savings**. Each one was used for at least 30 days by remote professionals.

## 1. 1Password — Password Management

1Password generates, stores, and auto-fills strong passwords. Encrypted generator, one-click auto-fill, secure sharing, breach alerts.

**Cost:** $2.99/mo (Individual).

<Callout type="tip">
Use the Watchtower feature to monitor for weak or reused passwords in real time.
</Callout>

## 2. Todoist — Task Management

Quick capture with natural language: "Call Sarah tomorrow at 2pm" — and it understands. Kanban, list, and calendar views. Integrates with 70+ tools.

## 3. Loom — Quick Video Messaging

Record your screen + camera with one click. Replaces meetings that could have been a 3-minute video. **Saves 3-5 hours per week.**

## 4. Momentum — Focus-Driven New Tab

A clean new tab with your daily focus task, motivational quote, and quick links. Focus Mode blocks distracting sites.

## 5. Ghostery — Tracker Blocking

Blocks ads, trackers, and pop-ups. Improves page load speed by 20-40%. Does not break sites like aggressive blockers.

## 6. Text Blaze — Text Expander

Create snippets with shortcuts: `!email` → your full email. `!sig` → formatted signature. Works in any text field.

## 7. Dark Reader — Universal Dark Mode

Apply dark mode to any site with adjustable brightness, contrast, and warmth. Preserves images.

## 8. Toby — Tab Organization

Save tab sets as sessions. Restore entire sessions with one click. Share collections with your team.

## 9. RescueTime — Time Tracking

Automatic time tracking across apps and websites. Most people discover 2-3 hours of daily time waste they did not realize existed.

## 10. Vimium — Keyboard Navigation

`f` → clickable links as overlays. `/` → search on page. `J`/`K` → scroll. Full browser control via keyboard.

<Callout type="warning">
Review extension permissions carefully. Only grant "all data on websites" to extensions that truly need it.
</Callout>

## The Golden Rule: Less Is More

Start with 3-4 that address your biggest pain points. Recommended order: 1Password → Todoist → Text Blaze → Ghostery.

## Bottom Line

The right extensions turn Chrome into a personalized productivity command center. Choose a few, configure them well, and master their shortcuts.
