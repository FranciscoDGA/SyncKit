## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Notion | Obsidian |
|---------|--------|----------|
| **Best For** | Team projects & databases | Personal knowledge & notes |
| **Data Storage** | Cloud-first | Local-first (Markdown files) |
| **Offline** | Limited | Full |
| **Collaboration** | Real-time, excellent | Not native |
| **Pricing (Personal)** | Free (limited) / $8/mo | Free |
| **Plugins** | Growing ecosystem | 1,000+ community plugins |
| **Privacy** | Data on their servers | Data on your machine |

## Architecture: The Fundamental Difference

**Notion** stores everything in the cloud. Your data lives on their servers, which means access from any device and real-time collaboration — but internet required and data subject to their privacy policy.

**Obsidian** stores everything locally as plain Markdown files. Full control over your data, works 100% offline, and files are portable.

<Callout type="info">
If privacy is a priority, Obsidian has a clear advantage. Your notes never touch a third-party server.
</Callout>

## Feature Breakdown

### Content Creation

| Feature | Notion | Obsidian |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Rich-text editor | Excellent, intuitive | Basic, Markdown-based |
| Databases | Native, powerful | Via Dataview plugin |
| Templates | Widely available | Via plugins |
| Tables | Drag-and-drop | Markdown + plugins |

### Organization

| Feature | Notion | Obsidian |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Hierarchy | Pages and sub-pages | Folders and sub-folders |
| Internal links | Wiki-style | Wiki-style (native) |
| Search | Excellent | Excellent (regex support) |
| Graph view | Not native | Visual connection map |

## The Plugin Ecosystem

**Obsidian** has 1,000+ community plugins: Dataview, Calendar, Kanban, Excalidraw, Tasks, Templater, and Periodic Notes.

**Notion** has a growing but relatively closed ecosystem. Most integrations come from the company itself.

## Performance at Scale

**Notion** can feel sluggish with 10,000+ pages. **Obsidian** is noticeably fast even with thousands of pages because everything is processed locally.

## Pricing

### Notion
- **Free:** 1,000 blocks, 5MB uploads
- **Plus ($8/mo):** Unlimited blocks, 100MB uploads
- **Business ($15/mo):** SAML SSO, advanced admin

### Obsidian
- **Personal:** Free for personal and commercial use
- **Sync ($4/mo):** Cross-device sync with E2E encryption
- **Publish ($8/mo):** Publish notes as a website

<Callout type="tip">
If you are a solo user, Obsidian is completely free. The $4/mo Sync plan is optional — use iCloud, Dropbox, or Git instead.
</Callout>

## When to Choose Notion

- You work on a team and need real-time collaboration
- You prefer visual interfaces and drag-and-drop
- You need powerful databases with multiple views

## When to Choose Obsidian

- You value privacy and full data control
- You work offline frequently
- You want deep customization
- You use linked thinking

## The Verdict: Use Both

Most power users run **Notion for team projects** and **Obsidian for personal knowledge management**. This combination covers each tool's strengths.

## Bottom Line

Notion is an exceptional collaboration tool. Obsidian is an exceptional thinking tool. Choose based on what you value most — or use both.
